NexaHaus Connect
Product preview · Phases 1–10

Static render of a subset of the screens, using the navy/gold design system and data shapes from the codebase. The backend now spans phases 1–10 (auth & RBAC, properties/units/leases, rent & payments, maintenance & work orders, inspections, owner statements, Property Health & Rescue, CRM, tenant & vendor portals, CSV import, notifications, a BullMQ worker, observability and CI/CD). Figures are representative demo data for the fictional diaspora owner Kwesi Mensah and five Accra properties from the seed script — not live. The running app needs Node + Docker installed.
